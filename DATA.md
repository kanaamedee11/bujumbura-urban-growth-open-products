# Data provenance and licences

`data/` holds four rasters exported by the author from Google Earth Engine with `bujumbura_urban_export.js` (a 22 km by 24 km box around Bujumbura):

* **Dynamic World V1** (Brown et al., 2022): dry-season (June to September) median built probability, 2016 to 2025, plus observation counts. Licence CC BY 4.0; attribution required: Dynamic World is produced for the Dynamic World Project by Google in partnership with the National Geographic Society and the World Resources Institute, and contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data.
* **ESA WorldCover** 2020 v100 and 2021 v200 (Zanaga et al.): CC BY 4.0, doi:10.5281/zenodo.5571936 and doi:10.5281/zenodo.7254221.
* **GHSL GHS-BUILT-S R2023A** (Pesaresi and Politis, European Commission JRC), doi:10.2905/9F06F36F-4B11-47EC-ABB0-4F8B7B1D72EA: open and free, source must be acknowledged (EC Reuse and Copyright Notice).
* **WorldPop** 100 m population (Tatem, 2017), **SRTM** elevation (NASA) and **Copernicus Sentinel-2** true-colour composites (for display only). Check each dataset's Earth Engine catalogue page for current terms.
